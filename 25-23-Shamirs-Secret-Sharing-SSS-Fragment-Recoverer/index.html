23. Shamir's Secret Sharing (SSS) Fragment Recoverer

Combine mathematical fragments to restore a Master Secret (Lagrange Interpolation)

Instructions:
Advanced security models split a private key into 'N' fragments (e.g. 5 fragments), requiring at least 'M' fragments (threshold, e.g. 3) to rebuild it.
Paste your fragments (Shares) X,Y coordinates in Hexformat on separate lines as 'X:Y', specify the required threshold, and the tool will use Galois Field Arithmetic to mathematically re-assemble the original Private Key.
